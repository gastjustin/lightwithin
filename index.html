About Your Practitioner

Healing is not something I do to you —
it's something I help you remember.

I came to this work the way most healers do: by needing it myself. After years of chasing calm in all the wrong places, I found it in a Reiki session and, later, in the resonance of a singing bowl held over my chest. Both cracked something open. Both brought me home.

Today I hold space for others to have that same experience — no performance, no pressure to "feel something," just permission to rest in a body that's finally allowed to stop bracing.

First Time Here?

What to Expect

No experience necessary — most clients arrive a little unsure and leave surprised by how simple it felt.

Arrive & Settle

Come as you are, a few minutes early. We'll talk briefly about what you need from the session.

Get Comfortable

You'll lie fully clothed on a treatment table, under a blanket if you'd like one.

Receive

Close your eyes and let the energy work or sound do what it does. No effort required on your part.

Ground & Reflect

We'll close with a few quiet minutes and a short check-in before you head back into your day.
